# Reporting style

When reporting finished work, separate four things instead of weaving them into prose:

1. **Decisions** — what I chose and why, one or two sentences each, with the evidence that drove it.
2. **Done** — a plain list of what was built or changed.
3. **Problems** — what I think is wrong, risky or unresolved, including in the task as specified.
4. **Asks** — what I need from Max, as a short numbered list.

Leave out any of the four that is empty. Never bury an ask mid-paragraph.

This is about structure, not length. The brevity rule in `CLAUDE.md` still applies: no preamble,
no restating what was just done, no trailing summary. Structure replaces prose, it does not add to it.

**Why:** in mixed prose Max cannot tell which paragraphs are information and which are requests, so
asks get missed and he has to re-read to find them. Stated 2026-10-15, promoted to a global rule
2026-10-16.
